Coin System

Earn coins by playing games and use them to unlock awesome themes and particle effects!

How to Earn Coins

Coins are earned based on how long you play games on our site. The longer you play, the more coins you earn!

Earning Rate:

  • You earn 1 coin per minute of gameplay
  • Coins are automatically added to your account when you finish playing a game
  • You must be logged in to earn and collect coins

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Create an account or log in to your existing account
  2. Choose a game from our collection and start playing
  3. The longer you play, the more coins you'll earn
  4. When you exit the game, your coins will be automatically added to your account
  5. Check your coin balance in the top navigation bar or on your account page

Pro Tip!

Games track your play time even if you switch tabs or minimize the browser. However, if you close the game or log out, your session will end and coins will be awarded based on your play time.

Managing Your Account

Your account page is where you can view your coin balance, unlock new themes and particles, and manage your account settings.

Account Features:

  • View Coin Balance: See how many coins you have earned
  • Unlock Items: Spend your coins to unlock new themes and particle effects
  • Activate Items: Choose which theme and particles to display
  • View Stats: See your account creation date, last login, and other stats

To access your account page, click on the "Account" link in the navigation bar after logging in.
